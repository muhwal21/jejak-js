# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want jejak.js to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience level, background or identity. Beginners asking basic questions are as welcome as experienced contributors.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are learning.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect differing opinions and experiences.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [@muhwal21](https://github.com/muhwal21). You can also use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour violates this code.
